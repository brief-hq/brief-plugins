# Brief Onboard

Load Brief product context for your company.

## Steps

1. Call `brief_get_onboarding_context` to load what Brief already knows about
   your company — product strategy, personas, active decisions, recent work,
   and velocity.

2. If Brief has no context yet, gather and share the following:
   - **What your company does** — product, market, stage
   - **Who your customers are** — personas, segments, recent feedback themes
   - **What you're working on now** — current priorities, open decisions, timelines
   - **Team context** — who's responsible for what, how decisions get made

   Send all of this to Brief in one call:
   `brief_ask "Here's what we're building and who we serve: [context]. What should I know?"`

3. Brief will respond with structured product context and its first insight.

## Usage

`/brief-onboard`
